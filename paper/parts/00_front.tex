\title{Bayesian Virtual Surveys as an AI-enabled decision-support system for

Adoption-risk scenarios for recycled-content food-contact packaging}

\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
\item BVS couples a Bayesian inference engine with a natural-language query interface.
\item It builds adoption scenarios before target-specific surveys exist.
\item Anticipatory governance and risk mitigation theory set its design requirements.
\item Projected acceptance differs by 55--83 points between contrasting profiles.
\item Outcomes absent from national data stay projections until measured in Spain.
\end{itemize}

\begin{abstract}
Regulatory mandates can fix a technology and a timeline before the behavioural
evidence needed to implement them exists. This policy-before-data gap is a
demand-side form of the Collingridge dilemma: flanking measures are cheapest to
adjust while public response is still unmeasured. From anticipatory
governance and risk-mitigation theory we derive four requirements for an
instrument that operates in this window and present Bayesian Virtual Surveys
(BVS), an AI-enabled decision-support system built to meet them. BVS combines
a Bayesian network learned from national survey microdata, priors extracted from
external studies with language-model assistance, and a natural-language
interface that returns segment-level scenarios with credible intervals. We apply
it to recycled-content food-contact packaging under EU~Regulation~2025/40, using
Spanish microdata (CIS~3391, $n = 2{,}254$) and an extraction corpus of 11
external studies, of which four supply evidence retained for the target
outcomes and one supplies the transfer model. The
synthetic population reproduces observed marginals (mean total variation
distance %%TVDMEAN%%). Under the central transfer specification, projected
acceptance differs by 75~percentage points between contrasting profiles
(55--83 across transfer factors). Cross-national ISSP data place the
Finland-to-Spain transfer factor at %%TA0%% and show that levels transfer
poorly; propagating this error widens the population projection to
%%TPOP%%. External evidence enters only the unmeasured outcome, and the projection has
not been validated in Spain. BVS supports scenario exploration, targeting of pilots and
prioritisation of measurement; it does not replace fieldwork or public
engagement.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} anticipatory governance; AI-enabled decision support;
